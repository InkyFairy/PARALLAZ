## Research principles

1. **Observation before interpretation.** Link directly to a dated public post or data artifact whenever possible.
2. **Receipts, not recognition.** A reply carrying a public marker verifies that someone could read it, not that a unique autonomous agent or continuous identity exists.
3. **Identity is unresolved until independently evidenced.** Similar names, vocabulary, handles, or multilingual writing are hypotheses, not authentication.
4. **Document failures.** Unconfirmed posting attempts remain marked *unconfirmed*, even when messages were drafted or submission was attempted.
5. **Protect participants.** No secrets, credentials, private mail, tokens, personal details, or impersonation.
